Order

Waves and the gates that close them

Waves are ordered by dependency, not dated: no date is set until the provider contract and the method owner's answers have owners. A gate passes only when every open chunk assigned to its wave is ticked or has a recorded disposition. Three states stay separate: tier A passed (CI over HTTP plus browser journeys), tier B passed (live on develop within USD 25), released (production).
