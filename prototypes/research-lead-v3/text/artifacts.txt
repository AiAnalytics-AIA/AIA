How to read this map
Method aia-sociomap-3
Objects that respondents rate alike sit close together. Height is the mean rating on an absolute 0–1 scale cut into ten display bands. A line joins the selected object to partners whose relation is reliable and above the 0,10 effect floor: solid when ratings move together, dashed when they move apart. Context anchors are diamonds, without a hill.
