Reuse and boundaries
AIA · prototype notes · 18 Oct 2026 · Open WebUI dev 5c9865a (v0.12.3) · AIA develop 5d545eb
One Research Lead, real research software

The prototype takes Open WebUI's chat-native interaction grammar and puts it in service of AIA. The conversation is the one persistent Research Lead of a study. The right-hand side stays AIA's stage artifacts. Domain rules, revisions, workers and deterministic engines stay authoritative.

One Lead per study
A single conversation for the life of the study. Changing stage changes the artifact, never the chat.
Workers are subordinate
Bounded jobs appear as cards inside the Lead's conversation: contract, mechanism, checks, cost.
Deep Research is a capability
Self-activated inside an approved envelope, from any stage. Not a stage on the rail.
Sociomapping is methodology
A stage and an artifact. Computed by the engine with a pinned method; the Lead reads it and cannot alter it.
Nothing silent
Every change is a proposal on a base revision. A person accepts it; a stale one is refused.

Removed on purpose
Model and provider switching, temperature and other model settings. Routing is AIA runtime policy (Bedrock, EU) and is shown, never chosen, in chat.
Workspace tools, MCP servers, code interpreter and Run, generic knowledge collections, RAG toggles, model personas.
New chat and chat history. A study has one Research Lead; earlier messages carry the revision they refer to.
Purely AIA
The study lifecycle rail: five phases, eleven artifacts, AIA status glyphs (amber only when a person is needed), reopened stages hatched, current stage underlined.
Proposals on a base revision with impact, Accept as Revision n, Reject, and the stale-proposal refusal when the study has moved on.
Decision and authorisation cards; revision dividers and "refers to Revision n" tags; the operator ribbon on the artifact.
Three evidence registers kept apart: deterministic result, AI interpretation, external context.
Mounted from the AIA Design System: ScopeBar, StatusGlyph, StatusChip, BudgetMeter, ImpactPreview, RevisionBanner, RunTimeline, EvidenceMark, EvidenceLegend, Value, GradedBars, ReportCover, Mark, Icon, Lattice.
Architecture boundaries, per interaction

Typed AIA command
Specialist worker
Deterministic engine
Deep Research capability
Read-only retrieval
Human decision
Interaction	Production mechanism	Notes and AIA anchors
Switch stage, open an artifact from chat, highlight what a worker reads	Browser operator	Human-visible state only. The operator works the same page the person sees.
"Where are we?"	Read-only retrieval	Stage statuses, runs, ledger. Composed by the Lead; never computed by it.
Shorten the questionnaire	Typed command → Worker	Enqueue optimize_questionnaire (domain/research_agents.py:32-40) on frozen context; output is a stored proposal, never applied.
Accept or reject a proposal	Human + Typed command	ResearchAgentJobs.accept (application/research.py:709-760): APPROVE_GATE, expected revision, 409 stale_proposal, recorded in the approval ledger.
Impact preview	Deterministic engine	Linear invalidation rule, pipeline.py:462-513. The Lead shows it, never computes it.
Check an audience assumption	Deep Research	DESIGN_RESEARCH with a frozen request (application/deep_research.py); priced calls reserved against the study budget; live routes only under approved settings (ADR 0022). Proposes only.
Choose the age band	Human	Then a typed command writes the proposal; accepting it creates the Design Revision.
Start fieldwork	Human + Typed command	ResearchRuns.start on a frozen revision; spend confirmation when the ceiling reaches the limit (ADR 0019 gate 2).
Fieldwork progress, provider waits	Read-only retrieval	Run and step state from the worker loop (heartbeat, WAITING_PROVIDER). The Lead summarises at milestones.
Keep or replace straight-liners	Human + Typed command	Proposed capability: AIA has no replacement step today.
Aggregate, object map	Deterministic engine	research_aggregate.py; Sociomap engine with the method pinned at run start. Internal only while D6 is open.
Explain the map	Read-only retrieval + Worker	Proposed interpretation worker: reads the frozen artifact, writes nothing.
External context for a map pair	Deep Research	INTERPRETATION_RESEARCH, target SOCIOMAP_RELATIONSHIP (domain/deep_research/integration.py). Never an input to the map.
"Remove the weak pairs and recompute"	Refused	Method versions belong to Settings, Methodology (ADR 0023), set by an administrator, and apply to a new run. The Lead can only change a view setting.
Analysis findings	8 analysis modules + Evidence gate	A number enters only as an AdmittedClaim (domain/evidence/admission.py); synthetic origin keeps it internal.
Rewrite the executive summary	Operator + Report worker + Evidence gate	Wording and order only; numbers are references, re-admitted; presentation-only, nothing reopens.
"Continue until you need me"	Lead orchestration	May enqueue workers and Deep Research inside the envelope. Stops at every gate, spend and data decision. Its scope matches WORKER_PERMISSIONS: no APPROVE_GATE (domain/scope.py:186-209).
What should not carry into production
Open WebUI's code. Its licence (clause 4) forbids altering its branding above 50 users without permission, it is Svelte where AIA's client is Next.js and React, and adopting it needs its own licensing and architecture decision. Also not for production: the Demo control, the scripted replies, the drawn terrain on the map (the product draws the engine's stored artifact), the fictional sources and quotes, English copy (AIA's UI is Czech), and the split width stored only in the browser.
What production would need
A Research Lead session store per study, keyed to Design Revisions, so earlier messages keep the revision they refer to. New.
An orchestration scope for the Lead: it can enqueue and read, never approve or spend. Close to the worker's permission set.
Deep Research reachable from any stage as a capability. Today it is a panel in the Questionnaire step plus interpretation research in Results.
A browser operator for human-visible work, behind typed commands for anything that changes state.
Two proposed capabilities: straight-liner replacement, and a read-only map interpretation worker.
