The plans that carry the build

From python tools/progress.py on 9 November, on develop: cost-model-study is planned, 0 of 32 chunks; client-data-in-analysis in progress, 1 of 15 (8a built); report-delivery in progress, 7 of 16; quantitative-families and situation-reports planned. The other plans the scope cites (decision-brief, deep-research-v2, public-data-routes, legal-context, sociomapa-systemu, research-findings-stage, analysis-frameworks, diagrams, register-personal-data) exist only on branch chore/pivot-business-decisions, with ADR 0046, Proposed and not merged; the scope's preamble says so and reads them at d8dc8ec5. The chunks the scope marks new exist in no plan file at all. ADR 0044 (quantitative models as a study kind) and ADR 0045 (reports composed per situation) are Proposed on develop. The scope (§ 2, § 7) maps each step to its chunks and orders them in five waves; this chapter's "Designed" column is that mapping.

Three honest statements

  • No business-decision report has been produced by AIA's product. A report's sentence "every number was computed by a verifiable program" can be true only of the program that computed it. The MVP's job is to make it true of AIA.
  • The canvas is design. The boards show the screens and the method; their numbers are fictional and illustrative, not validation evidence. The boards hold the method; the plan chunks hold the build.
  • The methods of the futures are not registered. Monte Carlo with PERT and correlations, the decision boundary, both measures of the value of information, the futures map and the weighting-scenario map await the methodologist's entries (chapter 14). Until then their results print odhad (metoda čeká na schválení metodikem), and a recommendation that rests on them is předběžné under the release policy the owner adopted on 9 November (chapter 2).

Not in this release

Cut from the ten weeks on 9 November, to keep the spine to what the first study type needs: the framework catalogue and picker in Zadání; model types other than site_cost_of_supply; automatic public-data and legal connectors (public inputs stay typed and sourced, entered by hand); fitted demand forecasting; global sensitivity by Sobol or PRCC; the respondent, expert, unit and period map modes; survey processing; the generic diagram editor; the shared client-review interface; monitoring. Each stays in its plan file as parked.
