Chapter 13

Grades, sources and the release gate

Every number in a business-decision report carries a grade in small type and a numbered source at the end of its chapter. The grade says how the number came to be; the source says where. Behind the one printed grade stand three more attributes.

The printed grade and the three attributes behind it

The printed grade says how the number came to be: its origin (z dat klienta, veřejný zdroj, právní předpis, úsudek, průzkum) or, for a model result, its method (výpočet, odhad). Three more attributes travel with every number and are never folded into the grade: its lineage (which inputs it was computed from and their grades), its uncertainty (the range, interval or probability basis, printed beside it) and its validation (the method in the register or awaiting the methodologist; a public input verified or prozatímní). A base-case figure computed from a judgement therefore prints výpočet, and its lineage shows úsudek, which the sentence must state where the judgement is decisive.

Sources, numbered per chapter

  • Each chapter ends with Zdroje a stupně: a numbered list; each printed number cites a list entry by its number ("z dat klienta 1").
  • An entry names the document by its cited name and page or paragraph ("Zápis zadání s klientem §6", "Dopis dodavatele, s. 1"), the file for data, the table for a computed value ("výpočet: tabulka v příloze"), or the method's status for an estimate ("odhad (metoda čeká na schválení metodikem)").
  • An entry may carry two grades when a number rests on both ("výpočet; z dat klienta: srovnatelné projekty"); the weaker is printed at the number.
  • The document register maps every cited name to its file, so a source is checkable with one open.
  • In AIA, the section-source manifest (domain/report/sources.py) already records each section's sources by artefact id and SHA-256; the numbered list is its printed form.

The provisional state

Prozatímní is a validation status, not a grade. It marks a public input not yet verified at the source, and it is printed in three places: the input table's state column, the sentence that uses the input ("dlouhodobý vývoj cen energie (prozatímní údaj)"), and the first-page state box with the count. The count comes from the manifest (chapter 2), never from the text. It is removed only by a dated verification by a named person. The report's running head carries Pracovní verze · prozatímní on every page while any decisive input is provisional.

The release gate (adopted 9 November 2026)

The owner adopted the release policy on 9 November: labelled unregistered estimates may appear internally and in an independently reviewed předběžné client draft; an approved recommendation needs registered methods, the independent review and every decisive comparison resolved. Document approval and recommendation eligibility are two states (chapter 2). This amends W27 (an unregistered estimate reaches the client report labelled, with a warning) and report-delivery RD0 of 22 October ("warnings for all of it") for the business-decision report: the warnings stay, and the recommendation's eligibility is a computed state beside them. The amendment is recorded in ADR 0046 by the docs PR that follows the scope's version 2.

  • Every decision-critical claim is checked twice: support in its source (code) and correct interpretation (a person who is not the claim's author).
  • A recommendation that rests on a method outside the register, or on a comparison still unresolved under the convergence rule, is předběžné doporučení, and its summary says so.
  • Exploratory material enters the report only with explicit limits ("exploratory; does not support the recommendation"). A map is not exploratory material: it supports the recommendation by showing why it holds and how robust it is, printed with its test result and limits, and never makes it.
  • Approval records the exact version and the open qualifications; "approved document" means reviewed and releasable, "recommendation eligible" means the recommendation may be stated as approved. The second is never typed; code derives it from the register, the review record and the resolution states.
  • The Deep Research thresholds (citation precision ≥ 90 %, unsupported claims < 10 %) are one check, not the release criterion.

What exists today: approval once per stored version with the approver and self-approval rules recorded (application/report_approval.py); the client gate's checks as warnings recorded on the approval's ledger row (domain/report/client_gate.py, RD0); the sentence check (RD7). What the policy adds: the recommendation's eligibility as a computed field of the approval, the qualifications list, and the second, human reading of each decision-critical claim.
