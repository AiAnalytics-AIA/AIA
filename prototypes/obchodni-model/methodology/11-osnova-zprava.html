Chapter 10 · Step 8

Osnova a Zpráva: the outline, then the report

The outline comes before the text. It lists every claim the report will make in the order of the argument, each with its source or method and why it matters to the decision. The report then says only what the outline holds.

What goes in

  • The results, the map, the input table, the findings table, the public inputs and the decision record: every artefact of steps 1–7, by id.
  • The part structure of a business-decision report and the grade and source conventions (below).
  • The validity review of chapter 14, before approval.

What comes out

The outline (Osnova zprávy)

Five parts in the order of the argument: 1 the decision and what is asked of the decision-makers; 2 why the preferred option is acceptable, or why none is; 3 what could change that conclusion; 4 implementation conditions, owners and dates; 5 supporting evidence and technical appendices. Each point carries its source or method, its grade attributes, and one line "why this matters" (proč na tom záleží). A judgement is a typed judgement record; a synthesis from sources is an inference record with its evidence. Stops (zastávky) close parts for review; a closed stop may go to the client's decision-makers for comment. The screen maps the five parts of the argument onto the report's parts, prints the seven grades with their four attributes, and opens a number's provenance card: source, method, uncertainty, validation, the run, the inputs it stands on, the data check behind it.

Every number carries one of the grades of chapter 13 in small type and a numbered source at the chapter's end; the summary's recommendation status says předběžné where the recommendation rests on a method outside the register (chapter 13). The report states its release state on its first page: Předběžné doporučení with each unresolved qualification named (unverified public inputs, a decisive judgement without a document, a reading awaiting confirmation, a failed stability test), and Schválit jako předběžné as the only approval offered while any remains. The summary may carry a paragraph Jiné vážení beside Jistota: which options win under the same weights of the perspectives, as a share of tested settings, never a probability, and not part of the recommendation.

Checks

  • No point without a source or a method; a point whose source was not captured does not enter the report.
  • The report says only what the closed outline holds. A sentence that compares in words with no number behind it is marked (RD7's sentence check, already built).
  • Every chapter's title is its finding ("No option yet passes the rule", not "Results").
  • Every number is a reference to a stored result: the same run, the same artefact id. Tables are generated by the model from the run, so they agree with the text by construction.
  • Counts the report prints (inputs, public inputs, judgements) come from the manifest; a count in the text that the table does not hold is a finding.
  • The required disclosures are in every report: the recommendation's status and qualifications, each number's source and method, what is outside the model, who stands behind the assumptions, the method versions.
  • The validity review is recorded before approval, and the approval records the exact version and the unresolved qualifications (chapter 14).
  • A stop closed with the client's people records their agreement (ADR 0046 W26).
  • No respondent, person or e-mail is named beyond a role; the report is Czech in the MVP (ADR 0046 W28).

Acceptance

  • Every number carries its grade and source: file and row or page, or the quoted public source.
  • An estimate from a method that is not approved is labelled odhad (metoda čeká na schválení metodikem).
  • The report is Czech, as PDF or DOCX, and every part above is present.
  • No respondent named or quoted by id.
  • Narrative, tables and figures do not contradict one another, percentile and probability wording included; this is checked by code and by a person.
