Appendix A

Glossary

TermMeaning in this methodology
Base case (základní případ)one computation with every input at its likely value; compares options and feeds sensitivity; not an estimate of the saving
Baselinethe option every other is compared with, often the status quo (keep buying, keep the current supplier)
Leafone cash-flow line (or lines that always travel together), discounted, allocated to exactly one perspective; a perspective's total is the sum of its leaves
Data contract (datová smlouva)which columns of a client file are used and how; units, currency, price year, personal columns, period; accepted by a person
Decision boundary (hranice rozhodnutí)what the rule chooses as one assumption moves over its range, the futures recomputed for each value
Dispositionwhat was done with a data finding: used; corrected with the reason; left out; taken as an assumption with a range
EVPPIexpected value of partial perfect information: the gain in mean outcome from knowing one input before deciding (Strong and Oakley 2013)
Fank perturbed weight vectors around a named weighting scenario, from a declared concentration; a sensitivity setting, not a probability
Futures (budoucnosti)the seeded draws of all inputs at once; probabilities are shares of them
Futures map (mapa budoucností)a scenario map whose rows are the futures and whose objects are the options; centred per future
Gatea non-compensable requirement; fail or unknown blocks the recommendation
Grade (stupeň)the small-type label on a printed number: how it came to be; chapter 13
Guarantor (stojí za ním)the person who stands behind an input's range
Interpretation recorda reading of a rule's condition that the rule does not fix, with the alternative, its effect and who confirms
Map contractper map configuration: what a row is, what the claim is about, what is measured, the test, the resampling, what the height means
Observed-data map (mapa z pozorování)a Sociomap whose rows were observed: experts' scores, comparable units or months; its test resamples by how the rows depend on each other
Oracle fixturea case with independently known answers (O-CF, O-INFO and the frozen test configuration) frozen by an analyst who does not write the model code and signed by the methodologist; the pipeline must reproduce it before any test case counts
Outline (Osnova zprávy)the report's claims in the order of the argument, each with its source and why it matters, before any text
Perspectivea set of leaves: financial, strategic, operational, marketing/commercial, logistics, environment and permitting, people; one weight per perspective, the same for every option
Recommendation eligibilitythe computed state that lets a recommendation be stated as approved: registered methods, the independent review closed, every decisive comparison resolved; apart from document approval
Repeated-look bounda confidence bound still valid after the draws have been doubled and checked several times; the convergence rule uses exact binomial bounds of this kind
Provisional (prozatímní)a public input not yet verified at the source
Register (registr metod)the methods the methodologist has approved, each at its version and fingerprint
Rule (pravidlo)the client's decision rule, written before any result: the measure, the choice, the conditions with thresholds
Scenario map (mapa scénářů)a Sociomap whose rows the model generates: futures or weight settings; tested by its stability across declared settings, never by p-values
Shared failurewhen one option is worse than the baseline, the share of those futures in which another is worse too, printed with its denominators
Share of tested settingsthe share of a fan's weight vectors under which an option is uniquely first, with the shares of ties and of unresolved settings beside it; per scenario, never pooled; not a probability
Stability testthe map recomputed under settings declared in advance; stable only if the distance orders agree above the threshold
Stopping rule (convergence)how many futures to run: 20 000, doubled at declared checkpoints up to 1,28 million, until every gate and decisive comparison clears its repeated-look bound; a comparison that does not prints as unresolved
Unresolved (nerozhodnuto)a comparison whose bound still crosses its threshold at the draws run so far; printed as an interval, never as pass or fail
Value for the rule (hodnota pro pravidlo)what knowing an answer in advance and applying the rule again would bring, averaged over the possible answers
Weighting-scenario map (mapa scénářů vah)a scenario map per scenario whose rows are tested weight settings and whose objects are the options; height is the score under the selected setting; it supports the recommendation, with the gates beside it and the rule's verdict in its own panel
