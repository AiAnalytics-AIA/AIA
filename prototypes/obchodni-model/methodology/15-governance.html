Chapter 14

Governance

Reproducibility proves the code computes what is written. It does not prove the model is the right one for the decision. That needs a review by someone who did not accept the inputs, a register that says which methods are approved, and a record of what is still open and whose word closes it.

The validity review (R1)

Before a recommendation is approved, an independent reviewer answers five questions; the researcher who accepted the inputs answers to them, never reviews them (board MV8 § 01; review R1).

The methodologist's open questions

ADR 0046 lists ten (§ Open: multiple testing on maps; levels or changes; effective sample size; resampling for 24–36 rows; the surrogate null; the relation statistic; the synthetic suite's thresholds; the discount rate in probabilistic rules; PERT inputs; forecast evaluation), with board MV5's recommendation for each. The exemplar and the scope (§ 8) add eight; board MV5 § 02 asks six of them with a recommendation each (PERT inputs; input correlations by Iman–Conover, a Gaussian copula or the common-plant draw; the stopping rule; the discount rate; whether pair statistics are read on the futures map; the stability threshold) and defers the unit and period modes' questions:

  1. The futures map's contract (R5, sociomapa-systemu#SYS9): rows are 20 000 chosen draws, so what does it print instead of the respondent method's "2 of 28 reliable" and split-half 0,91 against 0,59 (R2-P12 § 11.2)? The exemplar drew links by the -5 pair test with the false-discovery correction; board MV3's scenario contract says no null applies when the model creates the relations.
  2. The stability test (R2-P18 B): Spearman on the 28 distances between every two of three correlation settings, threshold 0,8; approve, or replace the statistic and the threshold. The "strong" setting (0,95 / 0,85 / 0,4) is the author's judgement.
  3. The empirical joint draw (cost-model-study#CR3b): one comparable plant per future carrying overrun and delay together, as an alternative to PERT with a declared correlation; whether 36 plants support it and how the 91 kt outlier is treated.
  4. The convergence rule and the endpoint semantics of chapter 2, adopted as the baseline on 9 November: confirm or amend the doubling from 20 000 to 1,28 million, the repeated-look exact binomial bounds, the 5 % error budget, quantiles fitted as quantiles and hard bounds as support; PERT λ; and when a mixture or a uniform is the better default for the 22 inputs the files do not hold.
  5. Dependence: the contract recommends a Gaussian copula for continuous inputs (validated rank-to-latent conversion, a valid latent correlation matrix, preserved empirical pairs, explicit event dependencies) in place of sample-wide Iman–Conover reordering, so the numerical error contract matches the sampler; confirm, and set the tail stresses and the achieved-dependence check.
  6. Value of information for the rule (cost-model-study#CR6b, R7): the rule re-applied per declared answer branch with the enquiry's cost, duration and shared dependencies inside the comparison; approve the definition, including its meaning when the rule's choice is "nothing", how it combines across several enquiries (the exemplar's 152,1 for all three authorities), and the estimator checks on the fixture O-INFO (17,5 for the rule, 12,5 as expected-benefit EVPI; § 14.6).
  7.   8. The seven print grades mapped to the code's (cost-model-study#CP8b, Q1): one table, Czech labels, admission rules.
  9. A register entry for aia-sociomap-5 with futures or settings as observers (today's entry covers respondents' ratings), or a separate method id per mode (sociomapa-systemu#SYS12).

Change propagation (K4)

A revised input is a new revision of the input table. Every result, table, map and approval that read the old revision is marked stale until someone reviews it; the report's version carries the revision it was composed from. In AIA today the pipeline's fingerprints and the impact rule (domain/pipeline.py, the research impact route) do this for a research design; the model platform reuses the rule (cost-model-study CP5: inputs frozen by artefact id and SHA-256).

Acceptance: the oracle first, ChemVitro second

A pipeline could pass the answer key with wrong cash flows: find the planted facts, add a charge for each trap, apply the rule faithfully to incorrect numbers, print the caveats, reproduce the same bytes. So acceptance has two layers (the owner, 9 November, R2-Q11). An analyst session that never writes the model code, and never imports it, freezes equations, inputs, expected intermediates, tolerances and conventions; the methodologist signs them (R2-Q12).

The tests the fixtures feed: units, currency and periods (the kilogram row hand-normalised to 2 844 t, the USD row at a frozen dated rate, a deliberately misclassified non-hazardous quote; silent substitution fails); accounting identities and scope (every expense once; zero usable tax capacity gives no immediate tax benefit); criterion splitting (a logistics line split with its total conserved leaves scores, ranks and shares unchanged; scoring parent and children together fails admission); missing-data states (known, evidenced zero, globally irrelevant, unknown range, several unknowns that jointly reverse a ranking; no site-specific reweighting); threshold uncertainty (known probabilities below, at and above a threshold; samples around 0,797 and 0,804; the whole stopping procedure under repeated seeds, not one final interval); correlated information branches (a finite joint-state table with impossible answer combinations; an independent-marginal substitution fails); distributions and dependence (analytic PERT moments, quantile-fitted marginals, paired observations, common national and site shocks); maps (known matrices, identical profiles, a dominated option with a similar profile, poor geometry, deliberate instability: similarity never becomes a co-winning claim); downstream invalidation (every stored root mutated, descendants stale, old artefacts immutable); claim meaning and release (wrong percentile wording, rounding that hides a gate failure, an unregistered method, an unresolved comparison stated as a verdict). The integration exit is one real browser, API and worker journey on the case files that produces the T1–T9 equivalents, both maps, the enquiry table and the QA annex from frozen artefacts and passes the independent review.

The independent review's evidence

Recommendation eligibility (chapter 2) needs a review record, not a reviewer's name. The record holds: the five questions of § 14.1 answered with the evidence each rests on; the limits the report admits with the check that caught each or the reason it is still open; the extreme-input tests actually run (the exemplar's chapter 14 lists seven computations that read as such tests, board MV8 shows them, and FND14 builds four; the register entry says how many a type-2 study must run and which); the oracle fixtures' pass record with the signer's name; the resolution state of every decisive comparison; and K1–K6 each marked met, partial or open with its anchor. A review with an open item leaves the document approvable as předběžné and the recommendation ineligible.
