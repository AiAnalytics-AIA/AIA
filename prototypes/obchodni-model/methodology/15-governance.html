Chapter 14

Governance

Reproducibility proves the code computes what is written. It does not prove the model is the right one for the decision. That needs a review by someone who did not accept the inputs, a register that says which methods are approved, and a record of what is still open and whose word closes it.

The validity review

Before a recommendation is approved, an independent reviewer answers five questions; the researcher who accepted the inputs answers to them, never reviews them.

#QuestionWhat the answer rests on
1Is every option represented comparably?the same scope, horizon, rate and timeline; option-specific months and costs from the option's own documents
2Are the material costs, benefits, constraints and implementation dependencies in the model?the scope check of every supplied estimate; the list of what is outside the model, stated where the model is described
3Could an excluded item reverse the recommendation?each exclusion's size against the margin of the verdict; an excluded item that could reverse it named on page 1
4Do extreme inputs give sensible results?the extreme-input tests run: each decisive judgement at the ends of its plausible range, the decisive price at an earlier level, each alternative reading of a condition, a delay with the decisive event ruled out; each behaving as expected
5Has an independent reviewer challenged the structure and the decisive assumptions?a named reviewer who did not work on the study, the date assigned, and the questions still open

With the questions, the review holds a table of the limits the report admits, each with the check that catches it and its state, caught or open; the statement that validity is not reproducibility; and the six review criteria with their state: comparable options with material costs and dependencies in the model; uncertainty fit for the decision; the rule's choice kept apart from supplementary scoring; a revised input propagating to everything that read it; every enquiry stating its effect on the deadline; and claim integrity between narrative, tables and figures. The review's record is part of the approval; its open items are the qualifications that keep the recommendation předběžné.

Register entries the method waits on

The Sociomap register (domain/sociomap_register.py) and Metodika's versions (domain/methodology/, ADR 0023) hold what is approved. A business-decision study needs entries that do not exist. Until each exists, its results print odhad (metoda čeká na schválení metodikem). NPV, IRR, payback, break-even and the tornado stay v registru: a single computation needs no entry.

The methodologist's open questions

ADR 0046 lists ten (§ Open: multiple testing on maps; levels or changes; effective sample size; resampling for 24–36 rows; the surrogate null; the relation statistic; the synthetic suite's thresholds; the discount rate in probabilistic rules; PERT inputs; forecast evaluation), each with a recommendation. This method adds ten:

  1. The scenario map's contract (sociomapa-systemu#SYS9): the owner's structure of 9 November reads no pair test on model rows and tests stability instead; approve it, and say which links, if any, the map draws without a pair test.
  2. The stability test: Spearman on the inter-option distances between every two of three correlation settings, threshold 0,8; approve, or replace the statistic and the threshold. The "strong" setting is the author's judgement and must be declared as one.
  3. The empirical joint draw (cost-model-study#CR3b): one comparable per future carrying overrun and delay together, as an alternative to PERT with a declared correlation; the minimum number of comparables that supports it, and how outliers are treated.
  4. The convergence rule and the endpoint semantics of chapter 2, adopted as the baseline on 9 November: confirm or amend the doubling from 20 000 to 1,28 million, the repeated-look exact binomial bounds, the mean bound for information comparisons, the 5 % error budget, quantiles fitted as quantiles and hard bounds as support; PERT λ; and when a mixture or a uniform is the better default for inputs the files do not hold.
  5. Dependence: the contract recommends a Gaussian copula for continuous inputs (validated rank-to-latent conversion, a valid latent correlation matrix, preserved empirical pairs, explicit event dependencies) in place of sample-wide Iman–Conover reordering, so the numerical error contract matches the sampler; confirm, and set the tail stresses and the achieved-dependence check.
  6. Value of information for the rule (cost-model-study#CR6b): the rule re-applied per declared answer branch with the enquiry's cost, duration and shared dependencies inside the comparison; approve the definition, including its meaning when the rule's choice is "nothing", how it combines across several enquiries, and the estimator checks on the fixture O-INFO (17,5 for the rule, 12,5 as expected-benefit EVPI; § 14.6).
  7. The weight settings under the partition (chapter 12): the value functions' anchors per perspective, the swing-weight elicitation, the fans at concentrations 20, 60 and 180 with 300 settings each, the robust-leader rule on score intervals, and the map-fit thresholds of its mode contract; shares are shares of tested settings, per scenario, never pooled.
  8. The seven print grades mapped to the code's (cost-model-study#CP8b): one table, Czech labels, admission rules.
  9. A register entry per configuration: aia-sociomap-5 with futures, settings, experts, units or months as rows (today's entry covers people's ratings), or a separate method id per configuration (sociomapa-systemu#SYS12).
  10. The observed-data configurations in the MVP: with units and months released with the rest, the ADR's questions that bear on them (multiple testing, levels or changes, effective sample size, resampling short series, the surrogate null, the relation statistic, the fixtures' thresholds) are answered before the maps are built; also what height means for units and months, and the number of rows below which a configuration does not map.

The risk the owner accepted. Short, autocorrelated series and small sets of units show patterns that are not there. Releasing the unit and month configurations with the rest, rather than after a separate validation suite, puts that risk inside the MVP. What still stands between such a map and a client: the configuration's planted-answer fixtures in the MVP's acceptance (§ 14.6), its register entry approved by the methodologist, and the release policy: a recommendation that rests on an unregistered map stays předběžné.

Settled by the scope: the MVP's spine is the business-decision study end to end; its model is a model type of its own, site_cost_of_supply (cost-model-study#CM8), inside the one merged study (#CP3: no new study kind). The accounting and market result views stay on the canvas as parked.

Change propagation

A revised input is a new revision of the input table. Every result, table, map and approval that read the old revision is marked stale until someone reviews it; the report's version carries the revision it was composed from. In AIA today the pipeline's fingerprints and the impact rule (domain/pipeline.py, the research impact route) do this for a research design; the model platform reuses the rule (cost-model-study CP5: inputs frozen by artefact id and SHA-256).

Acceptance: the oracle first, the test case second

A pipeline could pass a test case with wrong cash flows: find the planted facts, add a charge for each hidden cost, apply the rule faithfully to incorrect numbers, print the caveats, reproduce the same bytes. So acceptance has two layers (the owner, 9 November). An analyst session that never writes the model code, and never imports it, freezes equations, inputs, expected intermediates, tolerances and conventions; the methodologist signs them.

The tests the fixtures feed:

  • Units, currency and periods: a kilogram row normalised to tonnes by hand, a foreign-currency row at a frozen dated rate, a deliberately misclassified quote; silent substitution fails.
  • Accounting identities and scope: every expense once; zero usable tax capacity gives no immediate tax benefit.
  • Criterion splitting: a logistics line split with its total conserved leaves scores, ranks and shares unchanged; scoring parent and children together fails admission.
  • Missing-data states: known, evidenced zero, globally irrelevant, unknown range, several unknowns that jointly reverse a ranking; no option-specific reweighting.
  • Threshold uncertainty: known probabilities below, at and above a threshold; samples around 0,79 and 0,81 against 0,80; the whole stopping procedure under repeated seeds, not one final interval.
  • Correlated information branches: a finite joint-state table with impossible answer combinations; an independent-marginal substitution fails.
  • Distributions and dependence: analytic PERT moments, quantile-fitted marginals, paired observations, common regional and option-specific shocks.
  • Maps, every configuration: known matrices and planted groups (factors behind measures, a sparse relation between series); worlds with no relation (independent series, autocorrelated at 0,3, 0,7 and 0,95, seasonal); contamination (outliers, a level shift); identical profiles, a dominated option with a similar profile, poor geometry, deliberate instability. A configuration passes when it recovers what was planted and draws no more pairs than its error rate in the worlds with none; similarity never becomes a co-winning claim.
  • Downstream invalidation: every stored root mutated, descendants stale, old artefacts immutable.
  • Claim meaning and release: wrong percentile wording, rounding that hides a gate failure, an unregistered method, an unresolved comparison stated as a verdict.

The integration exit is one real browser, API and worker journey on the test case's files that produces every results table, the maps of both types, the enquiry table and the QA annex from frozen artefacts and passes the independent review.

The independent review's evidence

Recommendation eligibility (chapter 2) needs a review record, not a reviewer's name. The record holds: the five questions of § 14.1 answered with the evidence each rests on; the limits the report admits with the check that caught each or the reason it is still open; the extreme-input tests actually run (the register entry says how many a study must run and which; FND14 builds four); the oracle fixtures' pass record with the signer's name; the resolution state of every decisive comparison; and the six review criteria each marked met, partial or open with its anchor. A review with an open item leaves the document approvable as předběžné and the recommendation ineligible.
