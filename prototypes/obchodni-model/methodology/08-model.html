Chapter 7 · Step 5

Model: one computation for every option

The model computes the measure the rule reads for every option in the same futures. It is code, pinned by version and seed, and a rerun gives the same bytes. Nothing in it is a forecast or a legal opinion.

What goes in

  • The input table of step 4, frozen as a revision; the options and the baseline of step 1; the rule's measure.
  • The timeline every option shares: the decision date, permitting with each option's own extensions, construction with the drawn comparable's delay, ramp-up below full capacity for its first months.
  • The seed and the number of futures, or a stopping rule that sets the number.

What comes out

  • The base case: one computation with every input at its likely value. It compares the options and feeds the sensitivity analyses; it is not an estimate of what the client will save.
  • The futures: N seeded draws of all inputs at once, with their correlations, each future computing the baseline and every option. From them: the median, the 90 % interval, and every probability as a share of futures.
  • The extra computations on the same model and the same futures, each changing one assumption: the decision boundary over an assumption's range, delay of the decision, hiring delay, alternative terms, correlation settings.
  • The run record: model type and version, input revision, seed, N, the methods pinned, the artefacts with their SHA-256.

Checks

  • Every option is computed in the same futures by the same model; the baseline is one of them.
  • The same inputs and seed give the same bytes. This verifies the implementation, not the model's fitness for the decision (chapter 14).
  • The number of futures follows the convergence rule of chapter 2: 20 000 independent joint futures, doubled at declared checkpoints up to 1,28 million, until every gate and decisive comparison clears its repeated-look-valid bound; a comparison that has not cleared prints as unresolved. A fixed N and a ±0,5 pp margin alone certify nothing; 20 000 is the starting point, not the criterion.
  • Cash flows are discounted at the rule's rate, nominal or real as declared, with the convention named (mid-year or end-year; the inflation assumption and its state).
  • Tax is inside the model as a fixed parameter per jurisdiction, so the after-tax view agrees with every other view. The rule reads the pre-tax figure unless it says otherwise.
  • What is outside the model is listed where the model is described, not discovered in the limits: transfer prices, hiring time in the schedule, freight the delivered price already holds, the model's time step.
  • An extra computation changes one assumption; combinations that are not computed are said not to be.

In AIA today

  • Runs as workflows on the worker, with lease-fenced steps, artefacts, provenance and dependency edges, each artefact pinned by SHA-256 (apps/worker, infrastructure/artifact_repository.py, domain/workflow.py, domain/pipeline.py fingerprints).
  • A run pins the organization's methodology in force and records it in its identity (domain/methodology/pins.py, application/research.py).
  • The deterministic simulation core: a frozen WorldModel, reject-not-clip validation, seeded scenarios and frozen results (domain/simulation/). Its discipline is the model's; its content is not.
  • Seeded synthetic data from random.Random(seed) and the Sociomap golden fixtures pinned by SHA-256: the rerun-equals-bytes practice (packages/aia_core/tests/fixtures/sociomap/).
  • Budgets and cost ceilings per run, which a model computed by code does not need (a CALCULATED run has a known 0,00 USD ceiling, cost-model-study CP5).

Acceptance

  • First the oracle fixture O-CF (chapter 14): two years, 10 % discount, present costs 173,554 / 154,132 / 158,843 and a 12,1 receipt in year 2 worth exactly 10. Then the test case's seed and 20 000 futures as the first checkpoint; the same seed gives the same result.
  • A late start is bought at the spot price for the months between the end of the current terms and the plant's start.
  • Tax reliefs stay out of the pre-tax figure the rule reads; after-tax values use each jurisdiction's rate.
  • Every option shares the timeline from the decision date; an option's extra months (compensation, infrastructure, a permit) enter its own schedule.
  • The options are comparable, the material costs and dependencies are in the model, and a revised input invalidates the run's results and approvals.
