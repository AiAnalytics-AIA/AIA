Chapter 3 · Step 2

Data: the client's files under contract

Every client file is read, checked and bound by a contract before a number from it reaches the model. The checks produce findings, and every finding gets a disposition the report can print.

What goes in

  • The client's data files: for ChemVitro ten files in data/, from monthly purchases to air-quality measurements (CASE.md § 9). The worker survey belongs to type 3 and is not read.
  • The client's documents: seven PDFs, from the board's brief to the consultant's memo on air permitting. Each number taken from one is cited with its page.
  • The decision record from step 1, which says what each file is for.

What comes out

  • A data contract per file: which columns are used and how; units, currency and price year per column; which columns are personal and never leave the analysis copy; the period covered (board MV1; client-data-in-analysis chunk 2).
  • A quality report per file, accepted by a person before use: missing, duplicate and out-of-range counts per used column over every row (client-data-in-analysis 2a).
  • The findings table with dispositions. Every finding of the checks, with one of four dispositions: used; corrected, with the reason; left out; taken over as an assumption with a range. The findings that change the result or its reading go into the report (the exemplar's P1, R2-P18 Appendix B).
  • A document register: each document by the name the report cites it under and its file name (the exemplar's Appendix A).

A contract that names a column the file does not hold, or a quality report the person has not accepted, blocks the file from the model (MV0 M-1).

How the exemplar did it

The model uses every client file except the worker survey and all seven documents (R2-P04 § 3.4). The checks produced 42 findings, each with a recorded decision; the ones that change the result are in table P1 (Appendix B): the Hanshin price modelled as a range 1 470–1 694–1 868 EUR/t; the kilogram row; the single spot purchase of February 2026 turned into a spot premium of 20–60 %, most likely 39 %; the duplicate, the missing price year and the 91 kt outlier among the comparable plants; consumption growth of 4 % in the data against the client's 1–3 %, the client's range used; raw-material freight not added because the raw-material price is delivered to site; Odrzyca's rail quote and Eschfeld's bridge. Twenty-two inputs the files do not hold became judgements with a range. A separate document, Kontrola dat, holds the contracts and all 42 findings (Appendix C); the report prints the ones that matter.
