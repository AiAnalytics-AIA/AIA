Chapter 13

Governance

Reproducibility proves the code computes what is written. It does not prove the model is the right one for the decision. That needs a review by someone who did not accept the inputs, a register that says which methods are approved, and a record of what is still open and whose word closes it.

The validity review (R1)

Before a recommendation is approved, an independent reviewer answers five questions; the researcher who accepted the inputs answers to them, never reviews them (board MV8 § 01; review R1).

#QuestionIn the ChemVitro exemplar
1Is every option represented comparably?the same plant, horizon, rate and timeline; site-specific months and costs from the site's documents (R2-P02, Appendix B)
2Are the material costs, benefits, constraints and implementation dependencies in the model?the EPC exclusions priced; outside the model: transfer prices, hiring time in the schedule, raw-material freight, the annual step (R2-P15)
3Could an excluded item reverse the recommendation?raw-material freight would lower savings by 9–14 mil. EUR without changing the verdict (P1); the 2030 air limits could (R2-P01)
4Do extreme inputs give sensible results?the decision boundary 0–30 %, delays to 6 months, premium 0 and 30 %, three correlation settings (Appendix B); not run as named extreme-input tests

With the questions: four extreme-input tests with the expected behaviour written before they run (MV8 § 02), the statement that validity is not reproducibility (§ 03), and the six acceptance criteria K1–K6 with their state (§ 04). The review's record is part of the approval; its open items keep the recommendation předběžné.

The methodologist's open questions

ADR 0046 lists ten (§ Open: multiple testing on maps; levels or changes; effective sample size; resampling for 24–36 rows; the surrogate null; the relation statistic; the synthetic suite's thresholds; the discount rate in probabilistic rules; PERT inputs; forecast evaluation), with board MV5's recommendation for each. The exemplar adds five:

  1. Which contract governs the futures map. The exemplar drew links by the -5 pair test with the false-discovery correction and tested determinedness by split-half; board MV3's scenario contract says no null applies when the model creates the relations and asks for stability across seeds and settings instead. One of the two, or a stated combination.
  2. The stability test. The agreement measure over the 28 distances and the 0,8 threshold are the author's; the three correlation settings ("strong" = 0,95 / 0,85 / 0,4) are judgements.
  3. PERT from three judged points for the 22 inputs the files do not hold, and when a mixture or a uniform is the better default.
  4. The empirical joint draw of overrun and delay from 36 plants: whether a sample of that size supports it, and how a 91 kt outlier is treated.
  5. Value for the rule as a measure: its definition when the rule's choice is "nothing", and how it combines across several enquiries (the exemplar's 152,1 for all three authorities).

Change propagation (K4)

A revised input is a new revision of the input table. Every result, table, map and approval that read the old revision is marked stale until someone reviews it; the report's version carries the revision it was composed from. In AIA today the pipeline's fingerprints and the impact rule (domain/pipeline.py, the research impact route) do this for a research design; the model platform reuses the rule (cost-model-study CP5: inputs frozen by artefact id and SHA-256).
