Chapter 11

Perspectives, components and the two maps

The owner asked for every business perspective: financial, strategic, operational, marketing, logistics, "just everything". A perspective is a set of components of the decision; a component is one measure per option with a source, a grade and a direction. The components feed one new map, the weighting-scenario map, which sits beside the futures map and is never merged with it. This chapter takes its definitions and ChemVitro's components from the scope, § 3 and § 4.

Perspectives and components

A component is one measure per option, with a source (a model result, a client-data number, a cited finding, or a named expert's score), a grade (one of the exemplar's seven, chapter 12), a direction (↑ higher is better, ↓ lower is better) and a declared value scale (the worst and the best anchor as 0 and 10, linear between, clipped outside). A perspective is a set of components. The weighting-scenario map scores components; the client's rule does not read them. Board MV9 and tools/specs_weights.py (session D) realise the table below.

Five rules, from the review of 5 November:

  • Gates are not components (R2). The rule's four conditions are pass / fail / unknown per option. An unknown gate is "cannot be recommended until evidenced", and no weight compensates a failed one.
  • A missing or distributional figure keeps a range, never a point (R2). A country-level or comparables figure (the permitting extension, pre-treatment, the spur, the bridge, the trucking surcharge) enters as its range, the base the documented minimum; a component that does not apply to an option is null and takes no weight there.
  • One mechanism, one component (R3). Each component has one of three roles: úspora, the saving and its restatements (IRR, payback, after-tax NPV); v modelu, a part of the saving the model already holds; mimo model, what the model leaves out. The split is what lets the overlap check (§ 11.3, line 6) refuse a vector that weights the saving and one of its parts.
  • Judgement is labelled (A3). A component scored by a person carries that person's name and the grade úsudek.
  • An empty perspective stays and says so (ChemVitro's marketing; central for Velis). A sum row is not scored when its items are: the cost per tonne in 2032 is drawn by item, never as a total.

The futures map

Chapter 8 describes it. In one line each: rows are the model's draws; the claim is about the model; the cell is the rule's measure; centring per future makes the reading relative; the uncentred shared-failure table reads the absolute; links only where the mode contract allows; a stability test over settings declared in advance; relative apart from absolute; internal until the futures mode has its register entry.

The weighting-scenario map (Mapa scénářů vah, board MV9)

The owner's request: "allocate different weights to different components … whether logistics is a 0,3 and operations management is 0,5 … run the scenarios and display the scenarios on the Sociomaps." The ten lines of its contract, as the scope writes them (§ 4; chunk sociomapa-systemu#SYS13, new), and how session D's board MV9ScenarioWeights.dc.html realises each on the real engine:

  1. Objects are the options: the eight sites. The buy baseline is the rule's reference, not an object.
  2. Rows are named weight vectors over the perspectives, each with its family, composed by the researcher or the client's people ("LOG · b: logistika 0,3 · provoz 0,5 · finance 0,2"), each with who composed it and when, recorded in the approval ledger before results, by a composer who has not seen them (swing weighting, R3). On the board: six admitted scenarios in two families (below), composed as an illustration on 8. 11. 2026; a perspective's weight is shared equally by the components the family admits, and a perspective with no admitted component for an option takes no weight there.
  3. The fan around each row is k perturbed vectors from a declared concentration; it is a sensitivity setting, never a probability (R4). On the board: Dirichlet weights with concentration 60 (a standard deviation of about 0,04–0,06 per weight), 300 draws per scenario, seed 20261108, every other input at its base point, so the fan tests weights alone; 1 806 tested weightings in all. The map prints podíl testovaných nastavení, v nichž je možnost první, never P.
  4. A cell is the option's weighted score: each component read onto its declared value scale (the worse anchor 0, the better 10, linear between, clipped outside; cost-model-study#CS1), gates excluded and shown as pass / fail / unknown beside the map (R2). On the board the four conditions are gates G1–G4: today no site passes, so the map names nobody first after gates and shows preference only; after the authorities' written statement Lipová, Kamenec and Odrzyca would pass (chapter 7).
  5. A missing component keeps its range: the cell is an interval, the map is drawn at the documented minimum, and the row says what the top of the range would change (R2). On the board two ranges are decision-critical: at the top of Kamenec's permitting extension (9–15 months) it would lead EHS · b, and at the top of Bory's nearby hiring (0–380) Bory would lead the equal-weights scenario of family (b); each is marked as an evidence request.
  6. The overlap check marks components with one mechanism (§ 11.1, v modelu); a vector that weights the saving and one of its parts is refused by name and offered the two honest families. On the board the owner's "finance 0,5 · provoz 0,3 · logistika 0,2" over all components is shown refused: the saving already holds the operating cost and the freight. The same words in family (b), where finance means the investment and its ceiling, are the scenario LOG · b.
  7. Height is the weighted score under the scenario the reader chooses; relative (centred per row) and absolute readings side by side, as MV3's contract for the scenario mode (R5).
  8. Layout is the aia-sociomap-5 engine's over the rows × objects table; its pair statistics are not read, because the rows are settings we chose (MV3). On the board: 1 806 rows, the soft-rank Spearman layout, determinedness 0,95 against 0,59, three groups of rows found; Wendelstedt and Eschfeld share one place, so the renderer labels one. Options close together win under the same weightings.
  9. Apart from the futures map. The futures map's rows are the model's draws; this map's rows are preferences. They sit side by side and are never merged; MV3 calls MV9 the fifth reading of the same engine.
  10. Where it goes. The map is preference information. It enters the report under Co by závěr změnilo, never the recommendation; the client's rule decides (R3). In report 2 that is chapter 12 (P13) and one paragraph of the summary, Jiné vážení, graded like the rest (boards MV4 and M6, session E). Its method is a Metodika register entry of its own (sociomapa-systemu#SYS12), internal until the methodologist approves it, labelled metoda čeká na schválení metodikem wherever it appears.
