The business-decision study
A cost model of options

How AIA takes a client's decision from Zadání to a report in which every number carries its source and its grade. The method is described generically: it applies to any decision between options whose costs can be modelled.

The spine · eight steps

Zadání · Data · Rešerše · Předpoklady · Model · Výsledky · Sociomapa · Osnova a Zpráva

The client's rule is written at the first step and applied, as written, at the sixth. Every number in the report carries its source and one of seven grades. A method the register does not hold stays provisional, and a recommendation that rests on one is předběžné.
