Chapter 10

Built vs designed

No type-2 code is on develop. The exemplar's model ran outside the product. What AIA has is the ground the pivot builds on: the shell, the working copy, client sources, Deep Research, the Sociomap engine and its register, the report document model and its renderer, approvals and audit. This chapter says which is which.

The plans that carry the build

From python tools/progress.py on 8 November: cost-model-study is planned, 0 of 32 chunks; client-data-in-analysis in progress, 1 of 15 (8a built); report-delivery in progress, 7 of 16; quantitative-families planned, 0 chunks begun. ADR 0044 (quantitative models as a study kind) and ADR 0045 (reports composed per situation) are Proposed. ADR 0046 (the pivot) is Proposed on branch chore/pivot-business-decisions. The MVP scope (MVP-SCOPE.md, session A) maps each step to the chunk that builds it; this chapter must agree with it, and the coordinator folds in the scope when it lands.

Three honest statements

  • The exemplar was not produced by AIA's product. Its author computed the model with his own program and wrote the report by hand to AIA's conventions. The report's sentence "every number was computed by a verifiable program" is true of that program. The MVP's job is to make it true of AIA.
  • The canvas is design. The thirty boards show the screens and the method; their numbers are fictional, and the Velis case's numbers are illustrative, not validation evidence (board MV5). The boards hold the method; the plan chunks hold the build.
  • The methods of the futures are not registered. Monte Carlo with PERT and correlations, the decision boundary, both measures of the value of information, the futures map and the weighting-scenario map await the methodologist's entries (chapter 13). Until then their results print odhad (metoda čeká na schválení metodikem), and a recommendation that rests on them is předběžné under R9, which itself waits for the owner.
