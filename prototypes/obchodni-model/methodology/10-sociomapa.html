Chapter 9 · Step 7

Sociomapa: the futures map

A Sociomap places the options as points in a landscape: two options lie close when the same observers rate them alike, and the height shows the mean rating. In a business-decision study the observers are the model's futures. The map is evidence about the structure of the options' risks, never about the recommendation.

What goes in

  • The futures of step 5 as a table: one row per future, one column per option, the cell the measure the rule reads (the saving against the baseline in that future).
  • The mode contract for futures (mode Scénáře): what a row is, what the claim is about, what is measured, what the null means, the resampling, what the stability measures.
  • The stability test, declared before the computation: which alternative settings the map is recomputed under and what agreement counts as stable.
  • The method pinned: aia-sociomap-5, the organization's methodology in force.

What comes out

  • The futures map in two views: the 3D terrain and the top view that does not distort distances by perspective. Heights are mean saving; links are drawn only between pairs the contract allows; a warm link means co-movement, its width the relation's strength.
  • The relation matrix: each pair's relation across the futures, from which the map is drawn.
  • The shared-failure table: when an option is worse than the baseline, the share of those futures in which each other option is worse too, printed with its denominators (how many bad futures the share is of). This reads the absolute, uncentred futures; the map reads the centred relations, while its heights are the uncentred mean savings.
  • Three separate results, never one word: layout convergence (did the layout algorithm converge in every setting), representation fit (how well the layout represents the relation matrix) and robustness (the agreement of the distance orders across the declared assumption settings against the threshold). A grouping the report keeps after a failed robustness test is tested on its own, or the matrix is printed without a spatial reading.
  • The register status: the map's method at its spec fingerprint, approved or interní (domain/sociomap_register.py), printed on the map and in the report.

Checks

  • Mode contract. A row is one independent draw of the model. The claim is about the model and its assumptions, not about the market. Centring each future on the mean of the options makes the map show relative performance: who does better in the same future, not whether all do well.
  • Relative apart from absolute. The question "is option B a backup for A" is answered by the uncentred shared-failure table, not by the map: centring removes the common level, which is exactly what shared failure is made of. The table answers downside dependence only; whether one option could operationally stand in for another (switching time, cost, feasibility) is a separate assessment the report must name as such. A sentence like "no option is a backup of another" is a claim about tested downside dependence for the options it names, and prints with that scope.
  • Links only where the contract allows. Two rules compete. The signed-off method's pair test, with the false-discovery correction, marks pairs reliable; the futures mode's contract says a null makes no sense when the model creates the relations, asks for no p-value, and measures stability as the sensitivity of the shape to assumptions declared in advance. Which applies to futures is an open question for the methodologist (chapter 14); until it is answered, the report prints which rule it drew by.
  • Determinedness. Split-half agreement of two halves of the futures against the method's threshold says the layout is not an artefact of the draw. It does not say the layout is stable under the assumptions.
  • Stability, declared in advance. The map is recomputed under the alternative settings written down in step 4 (for correlations: none, the judgement's, strong) and counts as stable only if every pair of versions agrees in the order of the inter-option distances at or above the threshold (0,8 by default). A setting whose layout did not converge does not count as one of the settings until it does. Failure is printed, not softened.
  • What the map never claims: causation; the behaviour of the market or the firm; that a relation is observed rather than a property of the model; that a distance is an effect size in any unit; that the terrain between points is an estimate; validity borrowed from team-communication Sociomaps, which do not cover this use.
  • Internal until approved. Maps whose observers are futures or properties are an estimate awaiting the methodologist's approval; they print odhad (metoda čeká na schválení metodikem) and carry the internal label. The Sociomap of people's ratings is approved since 21. 10. 2026; this is a different mode.

New

  • The futures mode's contract (sociomapa-systemu#SYS9): rows a model's draws, each centred on the mean of the objects; the claim about the model and its assumptions, not the market or causes; no p-value; determinedness from the maps of two halves of the futures; stability as the shape's sensitivity to assumptions declared before the computation. It is the first of five readings of one engine (futures, experts, units, periods, and the weighting map as the fifth). The screen shows the map, the relation matrix, the shared-failure table, the relative and absolute readings side by side, and the stability test with its results and verdict.
  • The shared-failure table and the stability test: Spearman on the inter-option distances between every two settings ≥ 0,8, the settings declared before computing, a failure printed as it is (#SYS9b, new).
  • Each mode its own Metodika entry, internal until approved (#SYS12); the map on Výsledky and in the report (cost-model-study#CS5), declared extremes and direction per property (#CS1, reused by the weighting map).
  • The weighting-scenario map, a second map beside this one (sociomapa-systemu#SYS13, new; chapter 12). SI1–SI4 keep only what that map reads: states, gates, coverage, the overlap check.

Acceptance

  • The options placed by whether they do well in the same futures; links only where the declared rule allows; the view chosen and the reason stated.
  • The map labelled internal until Metodika approves the method.
  • The relation matrix and the shared-failure table printed; the backup question answered from the table.
  • The stability test declared before the computation, and its result printed whether it passed or failed.
  • The map is not part of the recommendation; the matrix, the text and the figure agree.
