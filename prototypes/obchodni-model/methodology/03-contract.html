Chapter 2

The analytical contract

Seven decisions have to be made before any model code is written, because each of them changes what a number in the report means. This version fixes a default for every one of them. The owner adopted the seven as the baseline on 9 November 2026; the methodologist reviews them on his first day and changes what he disagrees with. Until he does, the defaults bind every step.

The seven defaults

DecisionWho decidesDefaultWhat breaks if it stays open
Comparable-cost basismethodologist sets the basis; owner approves the business assumption; code enforces itFor every comparable: the original construction estimate, the actual expenditure on the same scope, the exclusions, currency and price year. Overrun = actual like-for-like cost ÷ original estimate − 1. Site-specific items (a rail connection, a bridge, pre-treatment) are added once, never inside the overrun as well. A source that does not say what its percentage measures is clarified in a new version of the source, on the like-for-like basis it states, before it is read as this ratio; the methodologist approves (the owner, 9 November; chapter 6).The investment, and any gate that reads it, has no defensible basis; a site item can be counted twice.
Complete parameter manifestcode owns the schema; methodologist approves uncertainty semantics; owner or client approves business assumptionsEvery value the model reads is a row: fixed values, ranges, structured tables, taxes and depreciation, tax capacity, timing dependencies, distributions, cross-option dependence, scales and method settings. Each row has unit, period, source locator, the grade attributes, an owner and its dependencies. A bound without a justification blocks every numerical claim that reads it. Counts in the report (inputs, public inputs, judgements) come from the manifest, never from the text.Hidden defaults escape provenance and invalidation; the text states a count the table does not hold.
Rule freeze and amendmentsowner or client confirms the meaning; code records and enforcesObjective, gates, rates, budget currency and year, the reading of each condition and tie treatment are frozen before any model result. A condition that can be read two ways (an investment ceiling as installed or as total investment, say) has its reading fixed and recorded. Exposure to earlier results is recorded. An amendment is a new revision with reason, approver, a rerun and the old and new outcomes disclosed.There is no authorised rule to apply; a result can be rationalised by a rule written after it.
Distribution endpointsmethodologist; owner or client supplies the business judgementsA hard bound defines the support; an elicited quantile is fitted as a quantile, never read as a bound. Reconciled observations keep their joint structure (a comparable's overrun and delay drawn together). Disagreement stays as separate scenarios or an approved mixture. Missing evidence keeps a justified range, never an automatic midpoint or a made-up distribution.Tail risk acquires a precision nobody stated; truncation hides the cases the gates exist for.
Numerical convergencemethodologist approves the error control; code chooses batching and resource limitsStart at 20 000 independent joint futures; double at declared checkpoints, capped at 1,28 million. Each gate gets a repeated-look-valid exact binomial bound; each median comparison an order-statistic bound; each decisive information comparison, which compares expected monetary gains, a repeated-look-valid bound for the mean that carries the conditional-estimation error of its answer branches (the estimator, an empirical-Bernstein or t-based confidence sequence for example, is the methodologist's choice). A probability or quantile bound never stands in for a mean bound: two enquiries can agree in how often they pay and differ in sign on average. The total numerical error budget is 5 %, split across comparisons and looks. A bound that overlaps the threshold means unresolved, and the report prints the interval. ±0,5 percentage points alone certifies nothing. Medians are compared as medians, not as the median of paired differences.A seed or a resource cap becomes the decision rule; 0,796 against a threshold of 0,80 on 20 000 draws (exact 95 % interval 0,790 to 0,802) is read as a verdict (chapter 8).
Enquiry updatingmethodologist approves the update; owner or client approves what counts as evidence and what action followsEvery enquiry declares its answer branches, their reliability, cost and duration ranges and the dependencies it shares with other inputs. The joint model is conditioned on the answer, the timetable shifts by the duration, the cost of asking enters, and the rule is reapplied. A letter from an authority does not remove all uncertainty by itself.The value of information cannot support an action or a deadline; the enquiry table is a wish list.
Release eligibilityowner sets the policy; methodologist registers methods; code enforces eligibilityTwo states, kept apart: document approval (this version was reviewed and may leave AIA) and recommendation eligibility (the recommendation may be stated as approved). Labelled unregistered estimates are allowed internally and in an independently reviewed předběžné client draft. An approved recommendation needs registered methods, the independent review and every decisive comparison resolved (adopted by the owner on 9 November; chapter 13)."Approved" means two different things to the client and to the methodologist.

The seven replace an implicit construction-cost uplift, a fixed N of 20 000 as an acceptance criterion and ambiguous release warnings.

Document approval is what application/report_approval.py records today, once per stored version. Recommendation eligibility is new: a field of the approval computed by code from the register, the review record and the comparisons' resolution states, never typed.

Who does what in the ten weeks

Three build sessions, one analyst session that never writes model code and freezes the oracle fixtures (chapter 14), and the methodologist one day a week, starting with the fixtures and these seven defaults (the owner, 9 November). The owner and the methodologist decide; a decision that has not arrived blocks the dependent result, not the engineering around it.
