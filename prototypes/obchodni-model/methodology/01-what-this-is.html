What the MVP produces

Study type 2, Model nákladů lokality: a decision between options, judged by a rule the client wrote before any result, on a cost model computed by code, with the uncertainty of every input carried through to the verdict. The exemplar is a type-2 report on a fictional client, ChemVitro, choosing among eight sites for a chemical plant or keeping its supplier. Its parts carry the ids R2-P01 to R2-P18 and its tables T1 to T9; this document cites them by those ids.

Types 1 and 3 of the case pack (the situation report without a model; the model beside a worker survey) are not the MVP. The survey lane is frozen (ADR 0046, decision 2).

How to read it

  • Chapter 1 is the spine: the eight steps in one table (the canvas's navigator shows nine; the outline and the report are one step).
  • Chapters 2 to 9 take one step each, all in the same shape: what goes in, what comes out, the checks, how the exemplar did it, what AIA has today, what is new, and what the ChemVitro acceptance expects.
  • Chapter 10 says, truthfully, what is built and what is designed. No type-2 code is on develop.
  • Chapters 11 to 13 cross the steps: the business perspectives and the two Sociomaps; grades, sources and the release gate; governance.
  • The appendices hold the glossary, the exemplar in numbers and the index of the canvas boards.

Czech words in italics are terms a person sees on AIA's screens or in the report. English words in code are files, modules or plan chunks in the repository. "The review" is the product owner's methodology review of 5 November 2026 (ADR 0046 § The methodology review, R1–R10, K1–K6). "The answer key" is prototypes/case-chemvitro/ANSWER-KEY.md. "The scope" is MVP-SCOPE.md, session A's document, whose wording this document takes as the source for the perspectives, the chunk ids and the acceptance A1–A6; where the two disagree, the scope wins and the coordinator is told. "The owner" is the product owner; "the methodologist" is the methodology owner, A. Bahbouh.

Three things this document does not do. It does not invent a number: every ChemVitro figure comes from the exemplar's tables or the case pack. It does not decide what waits on the owner or the methodologist; chapter 13 lists those items. It does not describe how a screen works: the boards do that.
