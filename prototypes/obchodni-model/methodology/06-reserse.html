Chapter 5 · Step 3

Rešerše: public inputs with a state

What the client's files cannot give, public sources give: prices, rates, taxes, limits and law. Each is a typed input with a quote, a source and a date, and it stays provisional until someone has verified it at the source.

What goes in

  • The list of public inputs the model needs, from the decision record and the contracts: market prices and their outlooks, regulatory thresholds and limit values, fees, wages and employer contributions, tax rates, depreciation and the nature of each incentive, energy price outlooks, exchange rates.
  • The client's data posture (Pravidla, domain/client_rules.py) and the study's budget, which bound what a priced route may spend.
  • Findings that frame the problem: the literature the report cites, found and quoted the same way.

What comes out

  • Typed public inputs: a value with its unit, the quote it was read from, the source and its date, the kind of source (Úřední / Oborový / Ostatní), the grade (veřejný zdroj or právní předpis) and the state: ověřeno once checked at the source, else prozatímní.
  • Findings by analysis: PESTEL, Porter or benchmark findings as proposals a person accepts, each a quote in a captured source.
  • The literature review as the report prints it: for each work, what it says and what follows for this report.
  • The state line for the report's first page: how many inputs are public, how many unverified, and whether the unverified ones could change the verdict.

Checks

  • A finding is a quote in a captured source, or it is nothing (ADR 0017). A value without a quote and a date is not an input.
  • A query's data class is inherited from what it was written from and never lowered by its words (ADR 0017, domain/deep_research/classification.py). A client's dataset never leaves as a query.
  • A provision is cited; no legal advice is given.
  • An unverified input is marked prozatímní in the input table, in the chapter that uses it and on page 1. Verification at the source is a dated act by a named person and changes the state, nothing else.
  • Every unverified input that enters the model says whether its range could change the verdict (chapter 8).
  • An incentive is read by its nature: a tax relief does not change a pre-tax figure; only a cash grant reduces the investment.

New

  • Typed inputs (value, unit, period, range, source, as-of date) extracted by code from accepted quotes and from structured routes, with the state prozatímní / ověřeno u pramene (deep-research-v2#DRV1 the typed input, #DRV2 the extractor, #DRV3 the extraction step); credibility tiers Úřední / Oborový / Ostatní (#DRV5); as a provenance of a model input, cost-model-study#CP1's public_source. Verification is a step with three outcomes: Deep Research finds and cites (prozatímní); the researcher opens the source and records the date and their name (ověřeno u pramene); what no source holds stays a judgement with a guarantor.
  • Structured public routes: SDMX for Eurostat and the ECB (public-data-routes#PDR1), ČNB rates (#PDR2).
  • A legal point as a verbatim provision that becomes a parameter (tax rate, depreciation, contributions): e-Sbírka and EUR-Lex routes, the legal point, legal parameters (legal-context#LGL1, #LGL2, #LGL3).
  • The first-page state line composed from the input table's states (part of the report recipe, research-findings-stage#FND13, chapter 10).
  • Findings by analysis (PESTEL, Porter, benchmark) as proposals, each a quote.

Acceptance

  • Every public value in the model carries a quote, a source, a date and a state; none is invented.
  • A regulatory threshold the test case puts an option above or below is read from its provision, and the cost it brings is in the model for every option it applies to.
  • Incentives are read by their nature; subtracting a tax relief from the investment is a misreading and fails.
  • Where a public range is a decisive input, the result says so; where the plan of action depends on verifying it, the verification has a date.
