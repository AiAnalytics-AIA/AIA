Chapter 9 · Steps 8 and 9

Osnova a Zpráva: the outline, then the report

The outline comes before the text. It lists every claim the report will make in the order of the argument, each with its source or method and why it matters to the decision. The report then says only what the outline holds.

What goes in

  • The results, the map, the input table, the findings table, the public inputs and the decision record: every artefact of steps 1–7, by id.
  • The part structure of a type-2 report and the grade and source conventions (below).
  • The validity review of chapter 13, before approval.

What comes out

The outline (Osnova zprávy, board MV4)

Five parts in the order of the argument (review R10): 1 the decision and what is asked of the board; 2 why the preferred option is acceptable, or why none is; 3 what could change that conclusion; 4 implementation conditions, owners and dates; 5 supporting evidence and technical appendices. Each point carries its source or method, its grade attributes, and one line "why this matters" (proč na tom záleží). A judgement is a typed judgement record; a synthesis from sources is an inference record with its evidence (review I5). Stops (zastávky) close parts for review; a closed stop may go to the client's decision-makers for comment.

Checks

  • No point without a source or a method; a point whose source was not captured does not enter the report (MV0 M-9; board MV4's point 3.4).
  • The report says only what the closed outline holds. A sentence that compares in words with no number behind it is marked (RD7's sentence check, already built).
  • Every number is a reference to a stored result: the same run, the same artefact id. Tables are generated by the model from the run, so they agree with the text by construction (R2-P18 Appendix D; K6).
  • The required disclosures are in every report: the recommendation's status and qualifications, each number's source and method, what is outside the model, who stands behind the assumptions, the method versions (board MV4's Povinná zveřejnění).
  • The validity review is recorded before approval, and the approval records the exact version and the unresolved qualifications (review R1, R9; chapter 13).
  • A stop closed with the client's people records their agreement (ADR 0046 W26).
  • No respondent, person or e-mail is named beyond a role; the report is Czech in the MVP (ADR 0046 W28).

How the exemplar did it

The report's first page is the summary in four paragraphs and the rule table; the second is the state of the inputs. Every chapter's title is its finding ("No site yet passes the rule", "The cheapest sites do not meet the deadline certainty", "The saving rests on today's price from Hanshin"). Every number carries its grade in small type, and every chapter ends with Zdroje a stupně, numbered. The part ids R2-P01 to R2-P18 let the executive comment part by part. The limits chapter admits what the report cannot claim: the compensation chance is a judgement, public inputs are provisional, the map is not stable, the methods of the futures await approval, each change was computed separately (R2-P15). The implementation plan is a dated table with owners and outputs (R2-P14). The tables T1–T9 are generated from the same run as the text (R2-P18).
