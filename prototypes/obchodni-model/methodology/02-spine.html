The spine

A study is eight steps. The client's rule is written at the first and applied, as written, at the sixth. Everything between them exists to make that application sound. Acceptance and waves

Acceptance has two layers. First an independent numerical oracle: toy fixtures with known answers (O-CF for the cash flows, O-INFO for the value of information) and a frozen test configuration, written by an analyst who never writes the model code and signed by the methodologist. Second a frozen test case with planted errors: data faults, costs a brochure hides and a site document reveals, decoys that look like problems and are not. The pipeline must find every planted fact, apply the rule as written, and reproduce its bytes; the case's numbers are not a target (chapter 14).

The build runs in five two-week waves (scope § 7): W1 contracts, oracles, the study shell, the rule freeze and the first data contract; W2 reconciliation, bindings and the cash-flow model; W3 finance, joint uncertainty and the rule with its convergence; W4 information value, the cost partition and both maps; W5 the report, invalidation, acceptance and the independent review. Three build sessions, one analyst session and the methodologist one day a week; 97–139 session-days in all. Week 10 may produce a reviewed předběžné report; the calendar does not turn an unresolved comparison into an approved recommendation.

The rules the whole spine keeps

  • The client's rule decides. It is written before any result and judged as written. Anything else the study computes is evidence for "what could change the conclusion", not for the recommendation.
  • A number is a reference to its origin. It carries a source, a method, an uncertainty and a validation status. A number the origin does not support is not printed.
  • Unknown is never good. A missing input keeps a range; a gate that is unknown blocks the recommendation until it is evidenced. A probability is a share of the model's futures, never a claim about the world.
  • Models propose, code computes, a person approves. A language model drafts text and suggests ranges; every number in the report comes from a program that gives the same bytes on a rerun.
  • A method the register does not hold stays provisional. Its results print odhad (metoda čeká na schválení metodikem), and a recommendation that rests on it is předběžné doporučení: document approval and recommendation eligibility are two states (chapter 2).
  • A comparison is resolved or it is an interval. The futures double from 20 000 at declared checkpoints until each gate and decisive comparison clears its bound; one that does not prints as unresolved.
  • Changes propagate. A revised input marks every downstream result and approval stale until someone reviews it. Every extra computation changes one assumption at a time and says so.
