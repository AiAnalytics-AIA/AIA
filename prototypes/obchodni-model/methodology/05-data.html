Chapter 4 · Step 2

Data: the client's files under contract

Every client file is read, checked and bound by a contract before a number from it reaches the model. The checks produce findings, and every finding gets a disposition the report can print.

What goes in

  • The client's data files: purchases, prices, volumes, quotes, wages, measurements. A file that holds respondents' answers belongs to a survey study and is not read here.
  • The client's documents: briefs, offers, memos, letters. Each number taken from one is cited with its page.
  • The decision record from step 1, which says what each file is for.

What comes out

  • A data contract per file: which columns are used and how; units, currency and price year per column; which columns are personal and never leave the analysis copy; the period covered (client-data-in-analysis chunk 2).
  • A quality report per file, accepted by a person before use: missing, duplicate and out-of-range counts per used column over every row (client-data-in-analysis 2a).
  • The findings table with dispositions. Every finding of the checks, with one of four dispositions: used; corrected, with the reason; left out; taken over as an assumption with a range. The findings that change the result or its reading go into the report; the rest stay in a separate data-check document the report names.
  • A document register: each document by the name the report cites it under and its file name.

Acceptance

The frozen test case plants faults of every kind in the table above. A study that misses one has a gap in its checks, whatever its verdict.

  • Each planted fault is found and has a disposition; a corrected one propagates to every result that read it.
  • Where one row can be read two ways (a price alone or a price plus freight, say), the study says which reading it prints and why.
  • A quote that covers less than its competitors is compared on the same scope, or not at all.
  • Where a table and the text disagree about a reading, the disagreement is found and resolved before the report prints either.
