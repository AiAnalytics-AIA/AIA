# Lišta (DockRail)

The graphite rail on every page: 64 px of icons at rest, 240 px on hover or focus, Settings' motion (off under reduced motion). Klienti, Společenská inteligence, Projektová paměť, Nastavení, then Můj Vedoucí as its own item with the researcher's Lead mark; the account at the foot. Nastavení opens the settings flyout. Navigation icons are the rail's own (UI-2 exception); their tiles take the wayfinding tints.

**Built** `components/aia/DockRail.tsx`, `dock-rail.css` (PBA1, PBA2). Open: the Nastavení flyout (PBA3).
