# Pohyb studií

- Every stage's forward action opens the next stage through the frame and focuses its heading (`FrameNavigation`).
- Every way out is a link, so a leave guard can stop it when there is unsaved work.
- The stage navigator marks a stage that the working copy reopened since the newest run's design ("změněno od běhu n"). Unknown marks nothing.
- The study bar and Přehled studie show what waits on you. The palette reaches any stage by name.
- A Lead focus or "@" point steps the conversation aside below 1100 px ("Zpět k Vedoucímu").
- Opening a stage never writes. Today opening Run saves a design revision; that is a defect to fix.


*Source: the interaction inventories of 27. 10. 2026, read from `apps/web` at develop@dbaa7720 (client workspace, Settings, shared controls, the workbench and its eleven stages). The anchors are in each rule.*
