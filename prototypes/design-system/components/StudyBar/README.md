# Přehled studie v liště (StudyBar)

One line under the stage navigator on every research stage. It holds:
- **where you are**: "Dotazník · krok 3 z 11";
- **what waits on you here**, as coral chips that open the item;
- **how many wait elsewhere** ("a 1 na jiném kroku");
- **what stands before the next run**: "Do spuštění zbývají 3 kontroly", or "Návrh se změnil od běhu 1".

It makes the process přehledný without scrolling: the start and the waiting decisions are one click from every stage.

**Rules**
- Nothing waits here: the calm chip "Na tomto kroku nic nečeká". Never empty space.
- The gate's words come from readiness (`…/research/readiness/working`), never from the client.
- Max one line at 1280 px. Chips beyond three collapse to "+ n".
