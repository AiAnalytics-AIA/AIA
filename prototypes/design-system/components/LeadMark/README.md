# Značka Vedoucího (LeadMark)

Each researcher's Lead has a mark, chosen on Můj Vedoucí → "Značka Vedoucího": Lissajous (default), Fourier, Slunečnice, Interference, Gauss, each with its formula. It is shown in the chat, on the timeline and in the rail, and applies to all of that researcher's studies; others have their own. "Pohyb značky": a calm mark stands still, and thinking then shows only the orbit. Owned by the Settings canvas (LOOK.md: the Lead's pages have one copy, Settings').

**The consumer provides** the researcher's chosen mark and motion setting; the mark sits on `shell` in the rail and the chat avatar.

**Replaces** `workbench/Icons.tsx:39` Mark (the AIA lattice used for the Lead) → the AIA mark stays the product's identity (Brand), the Lead gets its own mark. Not in the product yet.
